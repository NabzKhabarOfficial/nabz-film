The 2026 WrestleDream was a professional wrestling pay-per-view (PPV) event produced by All Elite Wrestling (AEW) and took place on October 17, 2026, at the Addition Financial Arena in Orlando, Florida, USA. Like previous events, it served as a tribute to New Japan Pro-Wrestling (NJPW) founder Antonio Inoki, who passed away on October 1, 2022. The event was headlined by the inaugural Clockwork Carnage match for the AEW World Championship, an eight-man elimination match culminating in a 15-minute race for the most falls.
