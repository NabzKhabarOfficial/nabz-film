TV Series

Mr and Mrs Sangi

2015

Comedy • Drama

The series tells the story of Saeed Sanghi's family's life. Saeed's father left a garden for the wedding expenses of his three daughters. Saeed sells the garden in order to start an ostrich farm. Ehsan Sangi, the grandson of their uncle, falls in love with Sahar (Saeed's younger sister) and intends to marry. Saeed, who sees the family in crisis, the only thought that comes to his mind is to put difficult and breathtaking conditions in front of Ehsan, apparently, Ehsan is a man of hard times and does not leave the field so easily!!! Now Saeed has only one way and that is to return the farm in any way before the situation is resolved by Ehsan, because otherwise what should not happen will happen.
