Valentina and the 12 Signs

2026★ 7.0

Comedy

Valentina is a young woman who, after being betrayed by her boyfriend—and blaming astrology for the end of their relationship—decides to take revenge in an unusual way: she sets out on a mission to hook up with a guy from every sign of the zodiac. Her plan is to document all these encounters and experiences in a videocast, but things get complicated when she falls for a guy who has "no sign."
