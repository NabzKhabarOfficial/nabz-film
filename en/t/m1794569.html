Unprotected: Murder in Galicia

2026★ 6.465 min

Documentary • Crime

Documentary film that tells the story of Isabel Fuentes. In April 2015, alleged burglars left her on the brink of death in her own home, in the village of Pazos, in Verín. From the very beginning, the Guardia Civil sergeant in charge of the case suspected that the account of what had happened did not add up and tried to protect Isabel from further attack.
