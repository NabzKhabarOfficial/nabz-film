The film was produced in 2025 (1404 in the Iranian calendar) and tells a story set against the backdrop of drought, focusing on a young woman and a teenager as they search for a hidden truth. The film features notable actors including Elnaz Shakerdoost, Mohammad Bahrani, and Danial Jafari.
