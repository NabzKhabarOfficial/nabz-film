Entertaining British sci fi adventure about killer plants from outer space. Loosely adapted from John Wyndham's novel, "Day of the Triffids." Remade twice including a TV mini series. Directed by Steve Sekely (& Freddie Francis uncredited) Released in 1963. a.k.a. "Invasion of the Triffids" Complete Print. TRIVIA: The producer listed in the credits, George Pitcher, had nothing to do with this film. He was a retired film executive whose name was used to meet a British legal requirement.
