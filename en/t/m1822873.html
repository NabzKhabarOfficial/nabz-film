Darius Rucker: They Called Me Hootie

202691 min

Documentary • Music

Darius Rucker gets deeply personal about the cost of stardom, revealing his struggles with drugs and alcohol, the breakdown of his marriage and brushes with death. Through candid interviews with collaborators, friends and family, new footage from the road and in the studio as he records his forthcoming album and never-before-seen tape dating back to the start of Hootie & the Blowfish, this documentary traces the full journey of his extraordinary career, revealing a deeply personal story of ambition, resilience and reinvention.
