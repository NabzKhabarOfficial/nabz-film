Drama • Family

Masoud is a conservationist who seems unhappy with his life with his wife, Mahtab. He has married his cousin at the insistence of his father, Haj Mahmoud, and is constantly at odds with her, which is why he decides to separate. Just when it seems that Masoud and Mahtab [who is a doctor] can come to an understanding in the midst of life's problems, something happens that affects their lives more than ever. While on duty in the heart of nature, Masoud shoots at illegal hunters, and the shooting injures one of the hunters. The hunter and Masoud are taken to the hospital where Mahtab works for surgery, and this incident creates a series of major problems for the Foruzesh family.
