My Love, the Black Sea

2026140 min

Drama

The series “Sevdam Karadeniz” tells the story of the enmity between two well-established families of the Black Sea region, which began with the unfinished love of Hafize Alazlı and Durali Yeniceli and, years later, drives their grandchildren Kuzey and Zeyşan into a forced marriage. Amid secrets, forbidden loves, and vows of revenge, the two young people are forced to make a choice that will change the fate of their families.
