The story follows an undefeated 70-year-old wrestler named Bhakhtaur Singh, who is burdened by family legacy. When his family lacks a male heir, he demands one, but his daughter-in-law Kammo chooses instead to defy patriarchal expectations by forming a girls' kabaddi team. The climax builds toward a major showdown against New Zealand's national team, pushing Bhakhtaur to recognize Kammo as his true successor.
