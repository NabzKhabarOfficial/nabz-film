UFC Fight Night 291: Buckley vs. Malott

2026

Action

UFC Fight Night 291: Buckley vs. Malott was a mixed martial arts event produced by the Ultimate Fighting Championship that took place on October 17, 2026, at Rogers Place in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. A welterweight bout between Joaquin Buckley and Mike Malott headlined the event. A women's flyweight bout between Erin Blanchfield and Jasmine Jasudavicius served as the co-main event.
