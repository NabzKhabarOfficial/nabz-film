Mr. & Mrs. Christmas

2026★ 10.084 min

Romance • TV Movie

Taylor Lewis, a single father and expert in holiday displays, moves to the picturesque town of Pineway with his two children just weeks before Christmas. While the family adjusts to a new home and school, Taylor also faces the realization that his teenage daughter may be growing out of some of their treasured Christmas traditions. When Taylor becomes involved in reviving Pineway's Christmas Market, he's paired with Sophie Shaw, a practical accountant eager to prove herself as a potential leader. Their different approaches soon complement each other, bringing new life and festive spirit to the town. As Taylor tries to hold onto the Christmas traditions his family once shared, he discovers that meaningful moments can't simply be recreated. Growing closer to Sophie along the way, he begins to see that embracing change can lead to new traditions, new memories, and perhaps an unexpected new beginning.
