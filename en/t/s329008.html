Drama

It is the story of "Hajj Mohammad Ali Zahedi" (known as Mandali), who has preserved the tradition of reciting Ta'ziyyah in Bastam for years. As his death nears on the eve of the month of Muharram, he leaves behind a will that brings his children together and causes deep financial and ideological differences between them.
