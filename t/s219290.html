درام

Dilan gave up all her dreams to marry Baran — a man from the family her family has been feuding with for years — to save her brother's life. Baran agreed to the marriage to stop the bloodshed, not to find love. What begins as a cold arrangement slowly becomes something neither of them planned for, while enemies circle from within and outside the family. A slow-burn daily romance built on blood feuds, reluctant hearts and glances that say more than words.
