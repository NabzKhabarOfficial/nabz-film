David and Goliath (1960)

📅 1960

کلاسیک

This ia a film adaptation of the Biblical story of the Israelite shepherd David, who became a hero and later king by his defeat of Goliath, the champion of the Philistines. It also deals with David's relation with the first king of Israel, Saul.
