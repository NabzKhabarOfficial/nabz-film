Kardeşlerim

📅 2021⭐ 7.8📺 4 فصل

خانوادگی • درام

Kardeşlerim follows four orphaned siblings struggling to survive after losing their parents. Thrown into poverty and hardship, they rely on each other while navigating school, work and dangerous social divides in Istanbul. As they face betrayal, love and injustice, powerful enemies repeatedly threaten their fragile stability. Blending emotional family drama with romance and conflict, the series explores loyalty, sacrifice and resilience in the face of constant adversity.
